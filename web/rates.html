How to read this chart & data sources

Each line is the asset’s daily close divided by its close on the hike announcement date: 1.20 means +20%, 0.80 means −20%. The study covers announced Fed tightening cycles since 1994. September midterm hikes covers 2018, 2022 and 2026. Cycles before the asset’s daily history are omitted.

The dashed historical average equally weights available prior cycles and excludes the latest event (September 2026). Hiding a line does not change the average. The hover readout shows the number of contributing cycles at each day. Closed markets carry the last close for up to four calendar days; lines stop at the latest actual observation. Moving averages use trailing calendar days.

Silver uses daily spot history from 2001, including the existing spot-adjusted futures continuation. The older weekly COMEX archive is not treated as daily prices. Returns are price returns, excluding dividends, fees and inflation.

Dates: Federal Reserve, historical archive, September 2026 announcement. Announcement dates can precede the effective date by one day.
